THE MATRIX - Rev. 3/9/98

by Larry and Andy Wachowski
NUMBERED SHOOTING SCRIPT  March 29, 1998

1 ON COMPUTER SCREEN
A blinding cursor pulses. A PHONE begins to RING.
CYPHER (V.O.) We're going to kill him. Do you understand that? He's going to die just like the others.
TRINITY (V.O.) Morpheus believes he is the One.
CYPHER (V.O.) Do you?
TRINITY (V.O.) I... it doesn't matter what I believe.

2 INT. HEART O' THE CITY HOTEL - NIGHT
Four police officers ready themselves outside room 303.
AGENT SMITH, AGENT BROWN, and AGENT JONES get out of the car.
AGENT SMITH No, Lieutenant, your men are already dead.
Trinity destroys the cops, flees across rooftops, dives through a window, races a garbage truck to a phone booth and vanishes.
AGENT BROWN The name is Neo.

12 INT. NEO'S APARTMENT
Neo asleep at his PC. A prompt appears: "Wake up, Neo." ... "The Matrix has you." ... "Follow the white rabbit." ... "Knock, knock, Neo."
CHOI buys a disc. Neo hides it inside Baudrillard's Simulacra and Simulations.
DUJOUR Come on. It'll be fun. I promise. (a white rabbit pin on her jacket) The ROOM TILTS.

13 INT. CLUB
TRINITY It's the question that drives us, the question that brought you here. You know the question just as I did.
NEO What is the Matrix?

20 INT. INTERROGATION ROOM
AGENT SMITH One of these lives has a future. One of them does not. A man who calls himself Morpheus. The most dangerous man alive.
NEO How about I just give you the finger. And you give me my phone call!
Smith seals Neo's mouth and implants a bug.

28 INT. ROOM 1313
MORPHEUS You take the red pill and you stay in Wonderland and I show you how deep the rabbit-hole goes.
Neo swallows the red pill.

39 INT. CONSTRUCT
MORPHEUS This is the world as it exists today. The desert of the real. The machines grow humans.
He holds up a coppertop battery. What is the Matrix? Control.

48 INT. DOJO
MORPHEUS Stop trying to hit me and just hit me.
MORPHEUS Do you believe that's air you are breathing now?
MORPHEUS I'm trying to free your mind, Neo, but all I can do is show you the door. You're the one that has to step through.

71 INT. MAIN DECK / RESTAURANT
CYPHER Ignorance is bliss. I'm tired, Trinity. I'm tired of this war.
AGENT SMITH Access codes to the Zion mainframe. Morpheus.

80 INT. ORACLE'S KITCHEN
ORACLE Know Thyself. Being the One is just like being in love. Nobody can tell you you're in love. You just know it. Through and through. Balls to bones.
ORACLE You're going to have to make a choice.

117 INT. ROOM 608
Morpheus sacrifices himself to Agent Smith so Neo can escape.

A171 INT. SUBWAY
AGENT SMITH That is the sound of inevitability.
NEO My name is Neo. (smashes Smith into the ceiling; the train barrels over the Agent)

211 INT. HALL
Neo stops the bullets in mid-air. He destroys Agent Smith from the inside.

219 ON COMPUTER SCREEN
NEO (V.O.) I know you're out there. I can feel you now. I know that you're afraid. I'm going to show these people what you don't want them to see. A world where anything is possible. To be free, you cannot change your cage. You have to change yourself.
Neo hangs up and flies into the sky.
THE END
