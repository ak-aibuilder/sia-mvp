THE MATRIX

Written by

Larry and Andy Wachowski

April 8, 1996


FADE IN ON:

COMPUTER SCREEN

So close it has no boundaries.

A blinking cursor pulses in the electric darkness like a heart coursing with phosphorous light, burning beneath the derma of black-neon glass.

A PHONE begins to RING, we hear it as though we were making the call. The cursor continues to throb, relentlessly patient, until --

MAN (V.O.)
Hello?

Data now slashes across the screen, information flashing faster than we read.

SCREEN
Call trans opt: received.
2-19-96 13:24:18 REC:Log>

WOMAN (V.O.)
I'm inside. Anything to report?

We listen to the phone conversation as though we were on a third line. The man's name is CYPHER. The woman, TRINITY.

CYPHER (V.O.)
Let's see. Target left work at 5:01 PM.

SCREEN
Trace program: running.

The entire screen fills with racing columns of numbers. Shimmering like green-electric rivets, they rush at a 10-digit phone number in the top corner.

CYPHER (V.O.)
He caught the northbound Howard line. Got off at Sheridan. Stopped at 7-11. Purchased six-pack of beer and a box of Captain Crunch. Returned home.

TRINITY (V.O.)
All right, you're relieved. Use the usual exit.

CYPHER (V.O.)
Do you know when we're going to make contact?

TRINITY
Soon.

CYPHER (V.O.)
Just between you and me, you don't believe it, do you? You don't believe this guy is the one?

TRINITY (V.O.)
I think Morpheus believes he is.

CYPHER (V.O.)
I know. But what about you?

TRINITY (V.O.)
I think Morpheus knows things that I don't.

CYPHER (V.O.)
Yeah, but if he's wrong --

TRINITY (V.O.)
Did you hear that?

CYPHER (V.O.)
Hear what?

SCREEN
Trace complete. Call origin: #312-555-0690

TRINITY (V.O.)
Are you sure this line is clean?

CYPHER (V.O.)
Yeah, course I'm sure.

TRINITY (V.O.)
I better go.

CYPHER (V.O.)
Yeah. Right. See you on the other side.

She hangs up as we PASS THROUGH the numbers, entering the netherworld of the computer screen.


INT. CHASE HOTEL - NIGHT

The hotel was abandoned after a fire licked its way across the polyester carpeting. We FOLLOW four armed POLICE officers as they creep down the blackened hall and ready themselves on either side of room 303.

The biggest of them violently kicks in the door. The other cops pour in behind him, guns thrust before them.

BIG COP
Police! Freeze!

Sitting there, her hands still on the keyboard, is TRINITY; a woman in black leather.

BIG COP
Get your hands behind your head!


EXT. CHASE HOTEL - NIGHT

A black sedan glides in through the police cruisers. AGENT SMITH and AGENT BROWN get out of the car. They wear dark suits and sunglasses even at night.

AGENT SMITH
Lieutenant, you were given specific orders.

LIEUTENANT
I think we can handle one little girl. I sent two units. They're bringing her down now.

AGENT SMITH
No, Lieutenant, your men are dead.


INT. CHASE HOTEL

The Big Cop reaches with the cuff and Trinity moves. The eye blinks and Trinity's palm snaps up and the nose explodes. She is inhumanly fast. She looks at the four bodies.

TRINITY
Shit.


INT. CHASE HOTEL

Trinity is on the phone, pacing.

TRINITY
Morpheus! The link was traced! I don't know how.

MORPHEUS (V.O.)
I know. Stay calm. There is a phone. Wells and Laxe. You can make it. Go.

She drops the phone, races to the fire escape, leaps rooftops chased by Agent Brown, dives through a window, and limps to a phone booth as a garbage truck bears down. She answers the phone and vanishes just as the booth is crushed. Agent Smith inspects the wreckage. There is no body.

AGENT JONES
Their next target. The name is Neo.


INT. NEO'S APARTMENT

A studio apartment overgrown with technology. We find NEO, a younger man who knows more about living inside a computer than living outside one. An anonymous message slices onto the screen.

SCREEN
Do you want to know what the Matrix is, Neo? Follow the white rabbit. Knock, knock, Neo.

Someone KNOCKS. It is ANTHONY, who lives down the hall, with friends, needing help with a Denver boot. Anthony's girlfriend DUJOUR wears a black leather jacket with a small white rabbit pin. The ROOM TILTS.

NEO
Yeah, yeah. Sure, I'll go.


INT. APARTMENT

An older Chicago apartment, a party. Neo stands alone until he notices a woman staring at him. The woman is Trinity. She walks straight up to him.

TRINITY
Hello, Neo. I know a lot about you. I brought you here to warn you, Neo. You are in a lot of danger. You came here because you wanted to know the answer to a hacker's question.

NEO
The Matrix. What is the Matrix?

TRINITY
Twelve years ago I met a man, a great man, who said that no one could be told the answer to that question. Once you see it, everything changes. The truth is out there, Neo.


INT. CORTECHS OFFICE

RHINEHEART, the ultimate company man, lectures Neo.

RHINEHEART
You have a problem, Mr. Anderson. You think that you're special. The time has come to make a choice.


INT. NEO'S CUBICLE

A FedEx man delivers a cellular phone. It RINGS the instant it is in his hand.

MORPHEUS (V.O.)
Hello, Neo. This is Morpheus. They're coming for you, Neo. Stand up and see for yourself.

At the elevator, Agent Brown and Agent Jones lead a group of cops. Morpheus guides Neo toward a scaffold and a window, but Neo loses his nerve and is taken.


INT. INTERROGATION ROOM

Smith sits across from Neo with a thick file: "Anderson, Thomas A."

AGENT SMITH
You have been living two lives. One of these lives has a future. One of them does not. We need your help bringing a known terrorist to justice. A man who calls himself Morpheus.

NEO
How about I give you the finger. And you can cram that file up your Secret Service sphincter.

Smith seals Neo's mouth shut and implants a fiber-optic bug in his navel.


INT. NEO'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

Neo bolts awake. Was it a dream? The PHONE RINGS.

MORPHEUS (V.O.)
This line is tapped, so I must be brief. They got to you first. Do you still want to meet? Take the Howard line south.


INT. TRUCK BED

A man named Cabie aims a gun. Trinity tapes Neo's eyes shut.

TRINITY
This has to be done for your protection and ours.


INT. VAN

A chop-shop. GIZMO and HACKSAW remove the bug with a hooked dental pick and a defibrillator after it wraps itself around Neo's intestine.

GIZMO
Nasty little bugger, ain't it?


INT. ROOM 1313

A dark figure stares out the windows. MORPHEUS turns.

MORPHEUS
At last. Welcome, Neo. Do you believe in fate, Neo?

NEO
No. Because I don't like the idea that I'm not in control of my life.

MORPHEUS
The Matrix is everywhere. It is the world that has been pulled over your eyes to blind you from the truth. That you are a slave, Neo.

He holds out a red pill and a blue pill.

MORPHEUS
You take the red pill and you stay in Wonderland and I show you how deep the rabbit-hole goes.

Neo swallows the red pill.


INT. POWER PLANT

Neo wakes in a womb-red amnion, bald and naked, plugged into a vast tower of pods. A machine disengages his cable. He is flushed into a waste line and snatched up into a hovercraft.

MORPHEUS
Welcome to the real world, Neo.


INT. HOVERCRAFT

Morpheus shows Neo the truth via the Construct: the year is closer to 2197, the machines grow humans for energy, the world is a desert of the real.

MORPHEUS
When the Matrix was first built there was a man born inside that had the ability to change what he wanted. When he died, the Oracle at the temple of Zion prophesied his return. That is why we have spent our entire lives searching the Matrix, looking for him.


INT. DOJO

MORPHEUS
This is a sparring program. Attack me.

Neo attacks; Morpheus blocks every blow.

MORPHEUS
Do you believe that's air you are breathing now? If you can free your mind, the body will follow.


INT. MAIN DECK / RESTAURANT (MATRIX)

Cypher dines with Agent Smith.

CYPHER
Ignorance is bliss. You get my body back in a power plant, reinsert me into the Matrix and I'll get you what you want.

AGENT SMITH
Access codes to Zion. Morpheus.


INT. TEMPLE OF ZION (MATRIX) / ORACLE

Neo meets the Oracle, a grandmotherly woman baking cookies.

ORACLE
You're not the One. Sorry, kid. You got the gift but it's tricky. Poor Morpheus. You're going to have to make a choice. One of you is going to die.


INT. HOTEL LAFAYETTE (MATRIX)

A deja vu, a black cat. It's a trap. Mouse is killed. The team flees through the walls. Morpheus sacrifices himself to Agent Smith so the others can escape.

MORPHEUS
Trinity, you must get Neo out. That's all that matters now.


INT. APPLIANCE STORE / MAIN DECK

Cypher betrays the crew, killing Apoc and Switch by pulling their plugs, before Tank, wounded, kills him.


INT. NIKO HOTEL (MATRIX)

Neo and Trinity assault the building to rescue Morpheus. Guns. Lots of guns. Bullet-time on the rooftop. Trinity learns to fly a helicopter.

TRINITY
Dodge this, motherfucker!

They save Morpheus.


INT. SUBWAY / EL STATION (MATRIX)

Neo faces Agent Smith alone.

AGENT SMITH
That is the sound of inevitability. It is the sound of your death.

NEO
My name is Neo.

Neo smashes Smith against the ceiling and the train barrels over the Agent.


INT. ROOM 303 (MATRIX)

Cornered, Neo is shot by Agent Smith and flatlines. On the ship, Trinity whispers that the Oracle told her she would fall in love with the One. She kisses him. His eyes snap open.


INT. HALL

Neo rises, stops the Agents' bullets in mid-air, and destroys Agent Smith from the inside. He dives into a phone as the EMP kills the sentinels attacking the ship.


FADE IN:

ON COMPUTER SCREEN

NEO (V.O.)
Hi. It's me. I know you're out there. I thought I should call and let you know how things stand. I've decided to make a few changes.

Neo hangs up, steps out of a phone booth, and flies up into the sky, his coat billowing like a black leather cape.

THE END
